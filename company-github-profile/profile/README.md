## About us

Exploree Solutions is developing **Exploree**, a connected digital platform that helps people discover relevant opportunities and gives organizations the tools to manage them.

We bring together employment, procurement, education, funding and professional events within one ecosystem. Our approach connects discovery with practical action: understanding requirements, preparing documents, submitting applications and managing the workflows that follow.

Our focus is Ethiopia, with services designed for individuals, businesses, educational institutions, development organizations and professional communities.

## Our purpose

We are building Exploree to make opportunities easier to discover, pursue and manage. By combining personalized recommendations, reusable profiles and digital workflows, we aim to reduce repetitive work and help people and organizations make better use of relevant opportunities.

## The Exploree ecosystem

Four connected modules form our product direction:

| Module | For individuals and participants | For organizations |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Exploree Jobs** | Discover relevant vacancies, prepare CVs and track applications. | Publish vacancies, manage applicants and coordinate recruitment and workforce activities. |
| **Exploree Tenders** | Find procurement opportunities, understand requirements and prepare bid documents. | Publish tenders and manage submissions, evaluations, approvals and procurement records. |
| **Exploree Opportunities** | Explore scholarships, grants, fellowships, internships and development programs. | Publish programs, collect applications and coordinate review, selection and participant administration. |
| **Exploree Events** | Discover professional events, register and manage participation. | Coordinate event publishing, registration, ticketing, admission and attendance. |

These modules describe the platform we are developing. Feature availability depends on implementation, testing and release readiness.

## Intelligence that supports action

The **Explore AI Engine** is designed to support the ecosystem through:

- **Relevant matching:** connect profiles, qualifications and interests with suitable opportunities.
- **Document intelligence:** help users understand requirements, deadlines and supporting materials.
- **Preparation assistance:** support CVs, applications and proposal drafts using user-provided information.
- **Timely recommendations:** surface relevant opportunities and reminders.
- **Authorized automation:** assist with supported tasks under user control.

AI assistance supports human judgment. Users and authorized organizational teams remain responsible for reviewing information, approving submissions and making decisions.

## One connected workspace

Our platform direction brings shared capabilities across the four modules:

- Individual profiles and reusable supporting documents.
- Organizational accounts and collaborative team workspaces.
- Role-based access and approval workflows.
- Notifications, saved opportunities and activity tracking.
- Subscription management and operational reporting.

This shared foundation is intended to reduce repeated data entry and connect the journey from discovering an opportunity to managing its outcome.

## Who we build for

- **Individuals:** students, graduates, job seekers and working professionals.
- **Businesses:** employers, suppliers, contractors and procurement teams.
- **Institutions:** universities, training providers, foundations and development organizations.
- **Communities:** event organizers, professional networks and participants.

## Our development approach

We focus on practical workflows, clear user control and a consistent experience across services. Our product direction emphasizes accessible discovery, permission-aware collaboration and human review of AI-assisted work.

Exploree's proposed service model combines free access with paid individual and organizational packages for additional assistance, capacity and management tools. Package details will be communicated through the relevant product channels as services become available.
